# Field photographs

Ten photographs documenting the study sites around metro station exits in
Haidian District, Beijing. They were taken by the author as part of the field
survey and are included to illustrate the on-site parking conditions described
in the thesis.

## Processing applied before publication

- Recognisable pedestrians, faces, vehicle licence plates, street-name signs,
  shop signage and uniform lettering have been **pixelated**.
- Images were downscaled to a 1600 px long edge and re-encoded as JPEG.
- **All metadata segments were removed**, including EXIF, GPS, XMP and ICC
  profiles. No capture timestamp, device model or geolocation remains in the
  files.
- Filenames were replaced with neutral sequential names (`field_01.jpg` …
  `field_10.jpg`) so that no millisecond capture timestamp is exposed.
